Help and support

If a signing request or validation fails, first note the action you were trying to perform and the exact error message, without copying private identification data or document contents into a public report.

  • For an invitation, an expired request, or a wrong recipient, contact the person who requested the signature.

  • For missing permissions, signing methods, or server configuration, contact your Nextcloud administrator.

  • For a reproducible LibreSign defect, consult the LibreSign issue tracker.

  • For project information and professional support options, see libresign.coop.

Include your LibreSign and Nextcloud version numbers in technical reports. Never publish signature passwords, private certificates, invitation links, or documents containing personal data.
